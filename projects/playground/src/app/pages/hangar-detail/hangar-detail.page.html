Hangar 4, Dubai South

About

A climate-controlled hangar on the business aviation side of Dubai World Central, two minutes from the FBO and customs. The 38-metre door and 11-metre clear height take anything up to a Global 7500 or a G700, and the floor is epoxy-sealed with ground power at every bay. Monthly leases include towing, a weekly exterior wash and 24/7 security with access logs; line maintenance from two approved organisations is on call.
